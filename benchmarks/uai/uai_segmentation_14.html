uai_segmentation_14

The 120 Markov and Bayesian networks of the UAI 2022 competition's MAR tuning set, each with its evidence; every marginal given the evidence; 300 s a run. aspmc, Ace, Storm, PRISM and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Made by gen_bn.py MAR_tune/Segmentation_14.uai --evid MAR_tune/Segmentation_14.uai.evid.

Seconds, a time limit of 300 s; >300: the time limit ran out. memory: out of memory. error: the system stopped with an error. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it.
