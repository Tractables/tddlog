uai_promedus_30

The 120 Markov and Bayesian networks of the UAI 2022 competition's MAR tuning set, each with its evidence; every marginal given the evidence; 300 s a run. aspmc, Ace, Storm, PRISM and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Made by gen_bn.py MAR_tune/Promedus_30.uai --evid MAR_tune/Promedus_30.uai.evid.

Seconds, a time limit of 300 s; >300: the time limit ran out. wrong: an answer off the reference by more than 1e-6. memory: out of memory. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.
