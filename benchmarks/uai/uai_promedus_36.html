uai_promedus_36

The 120 Markov and Bayesian networks of the UAI 2022 competition's MAR tuning set, each with its evidence; every marginal given the evidence; 300 s a run. aspmc and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use.
