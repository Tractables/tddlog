uai_or_chain_64_fg

The 197 networks of the UAI 2022 competition's final PR set: tddlog prints the probability of the evidence alone (--evidence-probability --no-queries) and Merlin and the other network tools run their partition function tasks; 300 s a run. Made by gen_bn.py PR_final/or_chain_64.fg.uai --evid PR_final/or_chain_64.fg.uai.evid.
