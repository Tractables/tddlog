uai_myciel5g_4_wcsp

The 197 networks of the UAI 2022 competition's final PR set: tddlog prints the probability of the evidence alone (--evidence-probability --no-queries) and Merlin and the other network tools run their partition function tasks; 300 s a run. Made by gen_bn.py PR_final/myciel5g_4.wcsp.uai --evid PR_final/myciel5g_4.wcsp.uai.evid.
