bn_insurance

34 Bayesian networks of the bnlearn repository, with and without evidence, one annotated disjunction per row of a table, every leaf queried; 300 s a run. aspmc, Storm, PRISM and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Made by gen_bn.py data/bn/insurance.net --query leaves.

Seconds, a time limit of 300 s; >300: the time limit ran out. wrong: an answer off the reference by more than 1e-6. skipped: not run: the system had failed on a smaller size of the same program. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

Psi (later run)

Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.

Not run in any repetition: not run: the system had failed on a smaller size of the same program.

Genfer (later run)

Genfer answers its own program: the Dice programs of its comparison in exact rationals, the population model in floating point (its masses summed over the bits of the circuit).
