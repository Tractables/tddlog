bn_earthquake

34 Bayesian networks of the bnlearn repository, with and without evidence, one annotated disjunction per row of a table, every leaf queried; 300 s a run. aspmc, Storm, PRISM and the Dice family ran in a later run. Made by gen_bn.py data/bn/earthquake.net --query leaves.

Psi (later run)

Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.

Genfer (later run)

Genfer answers its own program: the Dice programs of its comparison in exact rationals, the population model in floating point (its masses summed over the bits of the circuit).
