bn_hepar2_ev

34 Bayesian networks of the bnlearn repository, with and without evidence, one annotated disjunction per row of a table, every leaf queried; 300 s a run. aspmc, Storm, PRISM and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Made by gen_bn.py data/bn/hepar2.net --query leaves --evidence half.
