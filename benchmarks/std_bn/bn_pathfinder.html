bn_pathfinder

34 Bayesian networks of the bnlearn repository, with and without evidence, one annotated disjunction per row of a table, every leaf queried; 300 s a run. aspmc, Storm, PRISM and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Made by gen_bn.py data/bn/pathfinder.net --query leaves.

Seconds, a time limit of 300 s; skipped: not run: the system had failed on a smaller size of the same program. >300: the time limit ran out. wrong: an answer off the reference by more than 1e-6. Psi (later run)

Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.

Genfer (later run)

Genfer answers its own program: the Dice programs of its comparison in exact rationals, the population model in floating point (its masses summed over the bits of the circuit).
