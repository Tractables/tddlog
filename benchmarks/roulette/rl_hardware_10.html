rl_hardware_10

The scaling programs of the Roulette paper (Moy et al., PLDI 2025) on ladders of size, and its nine Bayesian networks. Roulette runs the artifact's programs; tddlog, Dice and Alea.jl run programs of the same models. On caesar Roulette's time is that of its query with #f, which keeps the answer. 600 s a run. Geni, noDice, Psi, Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs, the ProbLog programs or the networks); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. ProbLog, Geni, noDice, Psi, Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran in a later run, on other machines, on the instances the release run did not give them. Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; error: the system stopped with an error. memory: out of memory. >600: the time limit ran out. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A time is the median of the repetitions below, a failed one counted as infinite. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.
