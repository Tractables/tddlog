rl_diamond_1000

The scaling programs of the Roulette paper (Moy et al., PLDI 2025) on ladders of size, and its nine Bayesian networks. Roulette runs the artifact's programs; tddlog, Dice and Alea.jl run programs of the same models. On caesar Roulette's time is that of its query with #f, which keeps the answer. 600 s a run. Geni, noDice, Psi, Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs, the ProbLog programs or the networks); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. ProbLog, Geni, noDice, Psi, Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran in a later run, on other machines, on the instances the release run did not give them, each beside tddlog: their times compare with that run's tddlog, not with the other columns, and the comparisons below use it. Made by gen_roulette.py diamond 1000.

Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; >600: the time limit ran out. error: the system stopped with an error. memory: out of memory. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A time is the median of the repetitions below, a failed one counted as infinite. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

Roulette's own program of this benchmark, from its artifact (data/roulette/README.md): not a translation, the system's own program.

#lang roulette/example/disrupt
;; benchmark/modular/diamond.rkt of the Roulette artifact (Zenodo
;; 10.5281/zenodo.15253254), its last line changed to print P(s(N)) in
;; full (print.rkt).
(require "print.rkt")

(define (main n)
  (let go ([n n])
    (cond
      [(zero? n) #t]
      [else
       (define drop (flip 0.0001))
       (define route (flip 0.5))
       (define s1 (go (sub1 n)))
       (define s2 (if route s1 #f))
       (define s3 (if route #f s1))
       (or s2 (and s3 (not drop)))])))

(define SIZE (string->number (vector-ref (current-command-line-arguments) 0)))
(define result (time (query (main SIZE))))
(emit (format "s(~a)" SIZE) (result #t))
