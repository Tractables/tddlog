rl_hardware_1000

The scaling programs of the Roulette paper (Moy et al., PLDI 2025) on ladders of size, and its nine Bayesian networks. Roulette runs the artifact's programs; tddlog, Dice and Alea.jl run programs of the same models. On caesar Roulette's time is that of its query with #f, which keeps the answer. 600 s a run. Geni, noDice, Psi, Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs, the ProbLog programs or the networks); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. ProbLog, Geni, noDice, Psi, Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran in a later run, on other machines, on the instances the release run did not give them. Made by gen_roulette.py hardware 1000.

Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; memory: out of memory. skipped: not run: the system had failed on a smaller size of the same program. >600: the time limit ran out. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A time is the median of the repetitions below, a failed one counted as infinite. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

Roulette's own program of this benchmark, from its artifact (data/roulette/README.md): not a translation, the system's own program.

#lang racket/base
;; racket hardware.rkt FUEL: benchmark/scaling/hardware.rkt of the
;; Roulette artifact under rsdd with bit width 2 and n = FUEL - 2 (`-m rsdd
;; -p (2 n FUEL)`, the artifact's fuel ladder), its distribution printed
;; in full (print.rkt): res(T) for a total T, res(4) for #f (out of fuel).
(require (submod roulette/benchmark/scaling/hardware rsdd)
         roulette/example/private/pmf
         "print.rkt")
(define fuel (string->number (vector-ref (current-command-line-arguments) 0)))
(define result (main 2 (max 0 (- fuel 2)) fuel))
(for ([(v p) (in-pmf result)])
  (emit (format "res(~a)" (or v 4)) p))

Psi (later run)
Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.
