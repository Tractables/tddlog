rl_motivating_1000

The scaling programs of the Roulette paper (Moy et al., PLDI 2025) on ladders of size, and its nine Bayesian networks. Roulette runs the artifact's programs; tddlog, Dice and Alea.jl run programs of the same models. On caesar Roulette's time is that of its query with #f, which keeps the answer. 600 s a run. Storm and PRISM ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs or the ProbLog programs); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. Storm and PRISM ran in a later run, on other machines, on the instances the release run did not give them, each beside tddlog: their times compare with that run's tddlog, not with the other columns, and the comparisons below use it. Made by gen_roulette.py motivating 1000.

Roulette's own program of this benchmark, from its artifact (data/roulette/README.md): not a translation, the system's own program.

#lang roulette/example/disrupt
;; benchmark/modular/motivating.rkt of the Roulette artifact (Zenodo
;; 10.5281/zenodo.15253254), its last line changed to print P(x(N)) in
;; full (print.rkt).
(require "print.rkt")

(define (main n)
  (let go ([n n])
    (cond
      [(zero? n) (flip 0.1)]
      [else
       (define x (flip 0.2))
       (define y (flip 0.3))
       (if (go (sub1 n)) x y)])))

(define SIZE (string->number (vector-ref (current-command-line-arguments) 0)))
(define result (time (query (main SIZE))))
(emit (format "x(~a)" SIZE) (result #t))
