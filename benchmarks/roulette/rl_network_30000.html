rl_network_30000

The scaling programs of the Roulette paper (Moy et al., PLDI 2025) on ladders of size, and its nine Bayesian networks. Roulette runs the artifact's programs; tddlog, Dice and Alea.jl run programs of the same models. On caesar Roulette's time is that of its query with #f, which keeps the answer. 600 s a run. Geni, noDice, Psi, Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs, the ProbLog programs or the networks); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. ProbLog, Geni, noDice, Psi, Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran in a later run, on other machines, on the instances the release run did not give them, each beside tddlog: their times compare with that run's tddlog, not with the other columns, and the comparisons below use it. Made by gen_roulette.py network 30000.

Roulette's own program of this benchmark, from its artifact (data/roulette/README.md): not a translation, the system's own program.

#lang racket/base
;; racket network.rkt K: benchmark/scaling/network.rkt of the Roulette
;; artifact under rsdd (`-m rsdd -p (K)`), its distribution printed in
;; full (print.rkt): m(K,V) for a message whose bits, least significant
;; first, make V, m(K,16) for #f (lost). Roulette's distribution also holds #t
;; at a probability of 0 up to rounding; it is printed as other(#t).
(require (submod roulette/benchmark/scaling/network rsdd)
         roulette/example/private/pmf
         racket/format
         "print.rkt")
(define k (string->number (vector-ref (current-command-line-arguments) 0)))
(define result (main k))
(for ([(v p) (in-pmf result)])
  (cond
    [(not v) (emit (format "m(~a,16)" k) p)]
    [(list? v) (emit (format "m(~a,~a)" k (for/sum ([b v] [i (in-naturals)]) (if b (expt 2 i) 0))) p)]
    [else (emit (format "other(~a)" (~s v)) p)]))
