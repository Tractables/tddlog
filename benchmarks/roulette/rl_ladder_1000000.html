rl_ladder_1000000

The scaling programs of the Roulette paper (Moy et al., PLDI 2025) on ladders of size, and its nine Bayesian networks. Roulette runs the artifact's programs; tddlog, Dice and Alea.jl run programs of the same models. On caesar Roulette's time is that of its query with #f, which keeps the answer. 600 s a run. Geni, noDice, Psi, Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs, the ProbLog programs or the networks); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. ProbLog, Geni, noDice, Psi, Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran in a later run, on other machines, on the instances the release run did not give them, each beside tddlog: their times compare with that run's tddlog, not with the other columns, and the comparisons below use it. Made by gen_roulette.py ladder 1000000.

Roulette's own program of this benchmark, from its artifact (data/roulette/README.md): not a translation, the system's own program.

#lang roulette/example/disrupt
;; benchmark/modular/ladder.rkt of the Roulette artifact (Zenodo
;; 10.5281/zenodo.15253254), its last line changed to print the two
;; marginals o1(N) = P((#t #f)) and o2(N) = P((#f #t)) in full (print.rkt).
(require "print.rkt")

(define (main n)
  (let go ([n n])
    (cond
      [(zero? n) (list #t #f)]
      [else
       (define a (flip 0.001))
       (define b (flip 0.5))
       (match-define (list s1 s2)
         (go (sub1 n)))
       (cond
         [(and s1 a)
          (list #f #f)]
         [(or s1 s2)
          (list b (not b))]
         [else
          (list #f #f)])])))

(define SIZE (string->number (vector-ref (current-command-line-arguments) 0)))
(define result (time (query (main SIZE))))
(emit (format "o1(~a)" SIZE) (result (list #t #f)))
(emit (format "o2(~a)" SIZE) (result (list #f #t)))

Psi (later run)
Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.

Genfer (later run)
