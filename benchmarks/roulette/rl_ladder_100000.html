rl_ladder_100000

The scaling programs of the Roulette paper (Moy et al., PLDI 2025) on ladders of size, and its nine Bayesian networks. Roulette runs the artifact's programs; tddlog, Dice and Alea.jl run programs of the same models. On caesar Roulette's time is that of its query with #f, which keeps the answer. 600 s a run. Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs, the ProbLog programs or the networks); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran in a later run, on other machines, on the instances the release run did not give them, each beside tddlog: their times compare with that run's tddlog, not with the other columns, and the comparisons below use it. Made by gen_roulette.py ladder 100000.

A time is the median of the repetitions below, a failed one counted as infinite. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

Roulette's own program of this benchmark, from its artifact (data/roulette/README.md): not a translation, the system's own program.

#lang roulette/example/disrupt
;; benchmark/modular/ladder.rkt of the Roulette artifact (Zenodo
;; 10.5281/zenodo.15253254), its last line changed to print the two
;; marginals o1(N) = P((#t #f)) and o2(N) = P((#f #t)) in full (print.rkt).
(require "print.rkt")

(define (main n)
  (let go ([n n])
    (cond
      [(zero? n) (list #t #f)]
      [else
       (define a (flip 0.001))
       (define b (flip 0.5))
       (match-define (list s1 s2)
         (go (sub1 n)))
       (cond
         [(and s1 a)
          (list #f #f)]
         [(or s1 s2)
          (list b (not b))]
         [else
          (list #f #f)])])))

(define SIZE (string->number (vector-ref (current-command-line-arguments) 0)))
(define result (time (query (main SIZE))))
(emit (format "o1(~a)" SIZE) (result (list #t #f)))
(emit (format "o2(~a)" SIZE) (result (list #f #t)))
