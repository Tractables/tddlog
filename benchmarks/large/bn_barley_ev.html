bn_barley_ev

barley, diabetes, mildew and munin1 to munin4, with and without evidence, the large networks of the flip-hoisting evaluation (Cheng et al., 2021); 600 s a run. ProbLog, PITA, aspmc and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Made by gen_bn.py data/bn-large/barley.net.gz --query leaves --evidence half.

Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; >600: the time limit ran out. memory: out of memory. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

Psi (later run)

Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.

repetitionstatussecondsfrom the referencesizes
1timeout600

Genfer (later run)

Genfer answers its own program: the Dice programs of its comparison in exact rationals, the population model in floating point (its masses summed over the bits of the circuit).

repetitionstatussecondsfrom the referencesizes
1timeout600

SPPL (later run)

SPPL answers the continuous model, with normal emissions as densities; the circuit rounds them to a grid, so the differences from the reference are that discretization.

repetitionstatussecondsfrom the referencesizes
1timeout600

Geni (later run)

Geni computes in single precision and prints seven decimals, so differences of up to 5e-8 from the reference are its print precision.
