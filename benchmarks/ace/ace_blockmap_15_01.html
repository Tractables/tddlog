ace_blockmap_15_01

The 46 networks of Ace's benchmarks (Chavira and Darwiche), each with one of the archive's evidence sets; 600 s a run. ProbLog, PITA, aspmc, Ace, Storm, PRISM and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; skipped: not run: the system had failed on a smaller size of the same program. >600: the time limit ran out. memory: out of memory. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

Psi (later run)

Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.
