Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; memory: out of memory. >600: the time limit ran out. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.
