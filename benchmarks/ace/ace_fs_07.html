The 46 networks of Ace's benchmarks (Chavira and Darwiche), each with one of the archive's evidence sets; 600 s a run. Made by gen_bn.py ace-benchmarks/2006-ijar/fs-07.net --query all --evidence-file ace-benchmarks/2006-ijar/fs-07.inst.
