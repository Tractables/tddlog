s6

50 programs: random graphs, grids, smokers, UW-CSE, Cora citation matching, blood types and the Dice paper's programs translated to ProbLog; 300 s a run. aspmc, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI, TensorInference.jl and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Made by gen_smokers.py 6 --friends-per 2 --seed 1.

Seconds, a time limit of 300 s; A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

The programs

The files each system was handed, byte for byte: written by the release's scripts at the run's commit and captured by running the benchmark driver's own code with no system started. The first tab is the original program as its authors wrote it; each other tab is a translation of it into the language of the systems that read it, and says which script wrote it.

The original: the program as its generator wrote it, handed to tddlog, ProbLog -k ddnnf, ProbLog -k sdd, ProbLog -k fsdd, aspmc as it is.

Psi (later run)

Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.

Genfer (later run)

Genfer answers its own program: the Dice programs of its comparison in exact rationals, the population model in floating point (its masses summed over the bits of the circuit).
