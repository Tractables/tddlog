cora500

50 programs: random graphs, grids, smokers, UW-CSE, Cora citation matching, blood types and the Dice paper's programs translated to ProbLog; 300 s a run. aspmc, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI, TensorInference.jl, Storm, PRISM and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Made by gen_cora.py data/cplint/cora_m1.pl --queries 500 --seed 1.

A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.
