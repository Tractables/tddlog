Results › Probabilistic programs: Dice, Alea.jl, Roulette and further systems › The Dice paper's programs

bn_child

The programs and Bayesian networks of the Dice paper (Holtzen, Van den Broeck and Millstein, OOPSLA 2020), a network as one Dice program per queried variable, and its loop programs scaled up. tddlog runs them translated to ProbLog, Alea.jl translated to Julia, Roulette translated to Racket. 300 s a run (600 s on the loop programs). noDice, Psi, Genfer, Storm and PRISM ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs or the ProbLog programs); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. Storm and PRISM ran in a later run, on other machines, on the instances the release run did not give them, each beside tddlog: their times compare with that run's tddlog, not with the other columns, and the comparisons below use it. Made by gen_bn.py data/bn/child.net --query leaves.

Psi

Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.
