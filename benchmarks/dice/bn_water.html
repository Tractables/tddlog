Results › Probabilistic programs: Dice, Alea.jl, Roulette and further systems › The Dice paper's programs

bn_water

The programs and Bayesian networks of the Dice paper (Holtzen, Van den Broeck and Millstein, OOPSLA 2020), a network as one Dice program per queried variable, and its loop programs scaled up. tddlog runs them translated to ProbLog, Alea.jl translated to Julia, Roulette translated to Racket. 300 s a run (600 s on the loop programs). Geni, noDice, Psi, Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs, the ProbLog programs or the networks); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. ProbLog, Geni, Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran in a later run, on other machines, on the instances the release run did not give them. Made by gen_bn.py data/bn/water.net --query leaves.

Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; >600: the time limit ran out. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A time is the median of the repetitions below, a failed one counted as infinite. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.
