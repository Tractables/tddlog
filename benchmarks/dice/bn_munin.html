bn_munin

The programs and Bayesian networks of the Dice paper (Holtzen, Van den Broeck and Millstein, OOPSLA 2020), a network as one Dice program per queried variable, and its loop programs scaled up. tddlog runs them translated to ProbLog, Alea.jl translated to Julia, Roulette translated to Racket. 300 s a run (600 s on the loop programs). noDice, Psi and Genfer ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's or the Dice programs); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. Made by gen_bn.py data/bn/munin.net --query leaves.

A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A time is the median of the repetitions below, a failed one counted as infinite.
