nodice_hepar2

Cao et al.'s integer arithmetic programs (UAI 2023), Pluck's sequence models (PLDI 2025) and noDice's purely probabilistic programs (OOPSLA 2026). Alea.jl runs an Alea.jl program itself; tddlog and Dice run the Boolean circuit Alea.jl builds for it, and Roulette that circuit's Dice program translated to Racket. The noDice programs are Dice programs, translated, and Roulette runs them translated to Racket. 600 s a run. noDice, Psi, Pluck, Storm and PRISM ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs or the ProbLog programs); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. Roulette, Roulette's process, Storm and PRISM ran in a later run, on other machines, on the instances the release run did not give them, each beside tddlog: their times compare with that run's tddlog, not with the other columns, and the comparisons below use it. Made by dice2problog.py data/nodice/hepar2.dice.
