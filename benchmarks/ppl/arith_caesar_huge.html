arith_caesar_huge

Cao et al.'s integer arithmetic programs (UAI 2023), Pluck's sequence models (PLDI 2025) and noDice's purely probabilistic programs (OOPSLA 2026). Alea.jl runs an Alea.jl program itself; tddlog and Dice run the Boolean circuit Alea.jl builds for it. The noDice programs are Dice programs, translated, and Roulette runs them translated to Racket. 600 s a run. noDice, Psi and Pluck ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs or the ProbLog programs); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. The circuit of the Alea.jl program data/alea/arith/caesar_huge.jl.

Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; underflow: every answer printed as 0 where the probability of the evidence is below the smallest double. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A time is the median of the repetitions below, a failed one counted as infinite.
