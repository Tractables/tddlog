tpch_q21v_2

The TPC-H queries as Boolean queries over a tuple-independent database, at scale factors 0.001 to 0.1, flat and through views (208 instances); 600 s a run. Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI, TensorInference.jl, Storm, PRISM and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Made by gen_tpch.py 0.002 --queries q21 --seed 1 --views.

Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; error: the system stopped with an error. >600: the time limit ran out. memory: out of memory. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.
