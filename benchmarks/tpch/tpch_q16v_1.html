tpch_q16v_1

The TPC-H queries as Boolean queries over a tuple-independent database, at scale factors 0.001 to 0.1, flat and through views (208 instances); 600 s a run. Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI, TensorInference.jl, Storm, PRISM and the Dice family ran in a later run. Made by gen_tpch.py 0.001 --queries q16 --seed 1 --views.

Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; error: the system stopped with an error. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

Psi (later run)
