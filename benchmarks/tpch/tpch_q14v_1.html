tpch_q14v_1

The TPC-H queries as Boolean queries over a tuple-independent database, at scale factors 0.001 to 0.1, flat and through views (208 instances); 600 s a run. Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI, TensorInference.jl, Storm, PRISM and the Dice family ran in a later run. Made by gen_tpch.py 0.001 --queries q14 --seed 1 --views.

Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; error: the system stopped with an error. >600: the time limit ran out. memory: out of memory. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

Psi (later run)
Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.
