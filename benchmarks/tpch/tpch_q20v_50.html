tpch_q20v_50

The TPC-H queries as Boolean queries over a tuple-independent database, at scale factors 0.001 to 0.1, flat and through views (208 instances); 600 s a run. Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI, TensorInference.jl and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Made by gen_tpch.py 0.05 --queries q20 --seed 1 --views.

Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; skipped: not run: the system had failed on a smaller size of the same program. >600: the time limit ran out. memory: out of memory. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

Psi (later run)
Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.
