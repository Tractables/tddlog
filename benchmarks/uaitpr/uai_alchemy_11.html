uai_alchemy_11

The 121 networks of the UAI 2022 competition's PR tuning set: tddlog prints the probability of the evidence alone (--evidence-probability --no-queries) and Merlin and the other network tools run their partition function tasks; 300 s a run. ProbLog, PITA, aspmc, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI, TensorInference.jl and the Dice family ran in a later run. Made by gen_bn.py PR_tune/Alchemy_11.uai --evid PR_tune/Alchemy_11.uai.evid.

Seconds, a time limit of 300 s; error: the system stopped with an error. >300: the time limit ran out. memory: out of memory. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

Psi (later run)

Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.
