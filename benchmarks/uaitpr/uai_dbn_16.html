uai_dbn_16

The 121 networks of the UAI 2022 competition's PR tuning set: tddlog prints the probability of the evidence alone (--evidence-probability --no-queries) and Merlin and the other network tools run their partition function tasks; 300 s a run. Made by gen_bn.py PR_tune/DBN_16.uai --evid PR_tune/DBN_16.uai.evid.
