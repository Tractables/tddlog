uai_pedigree_11

The 121 networks of the UAI 2022 competition's PR tuning set: tddlog prints the probability of the evidence alone (--evidence-probability --no-queries) and Merlin and the other network tools run their partition function tasks; 300 s a run. ProbLog, PITA and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Made by gen_bn.py PR_tune/Pedigree_11.uai --evid PR_tune/Pedigree_11.uai.evid.

Seconds, a time limit of 300 s; >300: the time limit ran out. memory: out of memory. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

Psi (later run)

Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.

Genfer (later run)

Genfer answers its own program: the Dice programs of its comparison in exact rationals, the population model in floating point (its masses summed over the bits of the circuit).

SPPL (later run)

SPPL answers the continuous model, with normal emissions as densities; the circuit rounds them to a grid, so the differences from the reference are that discretization.

Geni (later run)
