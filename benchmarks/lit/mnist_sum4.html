mnist_sum4

39 programs of six families from published evaluations (mnist_sum, pedigree, sickness, noisy-or, mastermind, pdb), each grown on a random structure from a seed; 300 s a run. ProvSQL ran on the two tuple-independent families, pdb and noisy-or (the others are recursive or have annotated disjunctions), in a later run on another machine, each instance beside tddlog: its times compare with that run's tddlog, which its comparison uses. aspmc, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI, TensorInference.jl, Storm, PRISM and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Made by gen_mnist_sum.py 4 --seed 1.

Seconds, a time limit of 300 s; error: the system stopped with an error. memory: out of memory. >300: the time limit ran out. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

Psi (later run)

Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.

Genfer (later run)

Genfer answers its own program: the Dice programs of its comparison in exact rationals, the population model in floating point (its masses summed over the bits of the circuit).
