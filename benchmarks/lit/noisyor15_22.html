noisyor15_22

39 programs of six families from published evaluations (mnist_sum, pedigree, sickness, noisy-or, mastermind, pdb), each grown on a random structure from a seed; 300 s a run. ProvSQL ran on the two tuple-independent families, pdb and noisy-or (the others are recursive or have annotated disjunctions), in a later run on another machine, each instance beside tddlog: its times compare with that run's tddlog, which its comparison uses. aspmc, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI, TensorInference.jl and the Dice family ran in a later run, each instance beside that run's tddlog, which their comparisons use. Made by gen_noisyor.py 15 22 --seed 1.

Psi (later run)

Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.
