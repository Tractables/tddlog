geni_link_12

The programs of Geni's evaluation (Li and Zhang, ICFP 2025) that Dice runs: two grid networks and the weather factory, on ladders of size. ProbLog's d-DNNF and Roulette (on the Dice program translated to Racket) run beside the others here. 600 s a run. Geni, noDice, Psi, Storm and PRISM ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs, the ProbLog programs or the networks); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. Storm and PRISM ran in a later run, on other machines, on the instances the release run did not give them, each beside tddlog: their times compare with that run's tddlog, not with the other columns, and the comparisons below use it. Three more instances of the ladders no system ran: each system had failed on a smaller size. Made by gen_geni.py link 12.

A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A time is the median of the repetitions below, a failed one counted as infinite. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.
