geni_weather_n15

The programs of Geni's evaluation (Li and Zhang, ICFP 2025) that Dice runs: two grid networks and the weather factory, on ladders of size. ProbLog's d-DNNF and Roulette (on the Dice program translated to Racket) run beside the others here. 600 s a run. Geni, noDice, Psi, Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs, the ProbLog programs or the networks); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. Genfer, SPPL, Pluck, PITA, aspmc, Storm, PRISM, Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI and TensorInference.jl ran in a later run, on other machines, on the instances the release run did not give them. Two more instances of the ladders no system ran: each system had failed on a smaller size. Made by gen_geni.py weather 15 10.

Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; >600: the time limit ran out. memory: out of memory. zero: every answer printed as 0 where the reference's are positive. error: the system stopped with an error. skipped: not run: the system had failed on a smaller size of the same program. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A time is the median of the repetitions below, a failed one counted as infinite. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.
