The programs of Geni's evaluation (Li and Zhang, ICFP 2025) that Dice runs: two grid networks and the weather factory, on ladders of size. Geni, noDice, Psi, Storm and PRISM ran too, each on the programs it takes (its own paper's, the Dice programs or the ProbLog programs); a system's time is its process's, start-up included. Storm and PRISM ran in a later run, on other machines, on the instances the release run did not give them, each beside tddlog: their times compare with that run's tddlog, not with the other columns, and the comparisons below use it. Three more instances of the ladders no system ran: each system had failed on a smaller size. Made by gen_geni.py link 8.
