uai_segmentation_12

The 120 networks of the UAI 2022 competition's MAR tuning set: tddlog prints the probability of the evidence alone (--evidence-probability --no-queries) and Merlin and the other network tools run their partition function tasks; 300 s a run. Made by gen_bn.py MAR_tune/Segmentation_12.uai --evid MAR_tune/Segmentation_12.uai.evid.
