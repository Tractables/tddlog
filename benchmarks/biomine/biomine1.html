biomine1

The probability that each of four genes is connected to Alzheimer disease in the human Biomine network, over the union of the K most probable paths, for ten values of K up to 64; 600 s a run. Ace, Merlin, pyAgrum, pgmpy, libDAI, TensorInference.jl, Storm, PRISM and the Dice family ran in a later run. Made by gen_biomine.py biomine/human_3_oct_2018.zip 1.

Seconds, a time limit of 600 s; >600: the time limit ran out. A process time is the whole process's, with the language's start and compilation; the other time of the same system leaves them out, as its paper times it. A system run in several forms (ProbLog's knowledge compilers, Ace's routes) has the time of its fastest form.

Psi (later run)

Psi answers the Dice programs exactly, one run per component of the program's value, the times added. On its own linear regression it answers the posterior means of the continuous model, E(a) and E(b), which the circuit's bit queries do not have, so there is nothing to compare them with.

Genfer (later run)

Genfer answers its own program: the Dice programs of its comparison in exact rationals, the population model in floating point (its masses summed over the bits of the circuit).
